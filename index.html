07 — Featured Project

Project case study

FEATURED · IN DEVELOPMENT

BijliSOS — On-demand electrician service platform

Problem: Customers may find it difficult to locate an available electrician and agree on a service price.

Proposed solution: A platform concept where customers describe an electrical issue and budget, nearby electricians can respond with offers, and the customer can choose a suitable offer.

My role: Student project developer working on the Flutter interface and FastAPI backend.

Technology: Flutter, Dart, Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, SQLAlchemy, and Alembic.

Learning and challenges: Connecting a frontend to REST APIs, designing database models, implementing authentication, and testing backend endpoints.

Outcome: The project is under development; production release and real customer usage are not claimed.
